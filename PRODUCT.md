# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audiences are developers evaluating the products and investors evaluating the company. LafLabs presents primarily as a B2B software company, while individual products may also serve B2C users.

## Product Purpose

LafLabs builds software products and shared infrastructure across identity, payments, and cloud. The homepage should establish the company as a credible developer-led software business, lead visitors into its products, and make a direct inquiry the best secondary outcome.

## Positioning

LafLabs connects identity, payments, and cloud infrastructure into a coherent family of products built by one software company.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate LafLabs through its public products, technical explanations, open-source repositories, engineering principles, and direct company contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing products are Laf ID, Laf Pay, and LafDock.
- The homepage supports Korean and English.
- Product visits are the primary conversion path; company inquiries are secondary.
- The official domain is `laflabs.co`.
- The official contact address is `contact@laflabs.co`.
- Product claims, availability, testimonials, customers, and metrics must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Company name: LafLabs Inc.
- Blue is the primary brand color.
- Geometry remains square rather than rounded.
- The official symbol is the mark currently used by the LafLabs GitHub organization. The geometric logo in the existing frontend is not official and must be replaced.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing bilingual product and company copy in `lib/content.ts`.
- Existing product and repository links in `lib/content.ts`.
- Public company profile and official symbol at `https://github.com/laflabs-inc`.
- No confirmed testimonials, customer logos, investment metrics, or commercial performance claims are available.

## Product Principles

- Lead with concrete products and technical credibility.
- Make the company legible to both developers and investors.
- Keep the company identity broad enough for B2B infrastructure and product-level B2C expansion.
- Prefer verifiable product truth over generic marketing claims.
- Keep product discovery primary and contact consistently available.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic structure, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, readable Korean and English typography, and WCAG AA contrast.
